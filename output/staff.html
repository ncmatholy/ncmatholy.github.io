Our team

The people behind the problems.

Meet the leadership and test solvers who bring NC(J)MO to life.

These biographies are retained from the existing website. School years and time-sensitive details may need an update.

Organizing & problem writing

Leadership

Our leadership consists of experienced math olympians from NC. In addition to fulfilling our leadership roles, we all write problems to be selected for the tests.

AC

Alan Cheng

Co-Head of Durham Site

Alan is a junior from Cary, NC and attends the North Carolina School of Science and Math. He represented NC at MATHCOUNTS Nationals in 2021 and 2022, placed Top 5 at the NC State Math Contest in 2023 and 2024, and was invited to the Math Olympiad Summer Program (MOP) and Canada / USA Mathcamp in 2024. Alan is also a nationally acclaimed rock climber.

JL

Jason Lee

Co-Director

Jason is a homeschooled freshman from Durham, NC. Before NCMO, he started the Winter Math Competitions and ran it from 2021 to 2024. He also inspired the creation of the Washington Math Olympiad, testsolved for Ze Math Competitions, and wrote the power round of the ASDAN Math Tournament in 2022. Outside of math, Jason enjoys music, robotics, mock trial, teaching, and attempting to write.

GP

Grisham Paimagam

Co-Director and Head of Charlotte Site

Grisham is a sophomore from Charlotte, NC and attends Myers Park High School, where he is the math club head. He has been actively participating in math contests for around 3 years, and has recently started math research as well. Outside of math, Grisham is an avid squash player and is top 50 for his age group nationally, and loves to play basketball and football for fun.

AW

Aaron Wang

Co-Head of Durham Site

Aaron is a junior from Charlotte, NC and attends the North Carolina School of Science and Math. He is famous for rederiving the circumcenter of a right triangle in his (correct) in-contest solution to USAMO 2024/5, which just so happened to qualify him for MOP. Aaron enjoys playing tennis and piano.

Test solver

BW

Test solver

He also founded and directed the aforementioned Ze Math Competitions, and currently writes for his school's competition. Twice a year, Benny solves an AMC problem on his youtube channel.
